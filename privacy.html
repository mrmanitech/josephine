Josephine privacy policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Josephine is a private app used by one family. Only Google accounts on the family's own list can use it.

What it accesses

  • Your Google account email, so it can check that you are on the family list.
  • Google Drive, limited to files Josephine creates (the drive.file scope). It cannot see or change anything else in Drive.

Where data is stored

Photos, videos, notes and captions are stored only in the family owner's own Google Drive. Nothing is stored on any other server.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared with anyone, and there are no ads or analytics. Google user data is used only to provide the app's features.

Deleting data

Delete items in the app, or delete the Josephine folder in Google Drive. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
